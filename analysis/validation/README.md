# Validation

The label scheme was validated on a stratified subsample of 120 preprint-
publication pairs. Each pair was labelled by two independent domain experts
(H.Y. and R.R.) and by a five-call model panel: Claude Haiku, Claude Opus, and
three replicate runs of Claude Sonnet.

Agreement on whether the primary claim materially changed (Cohen's kappa) was
0.63 to 0.66 between the model and the experts, 0.60 between the two experts, and
0.75 across the three Sonnet replicates. On the basis of the replicate agreement,
the single Sonnet scheme was applied to the full corpus.
